A signed atomic sequence of `LiquidatePosition` followed by `ProcessPayout` supplying the liquidated Position as `claim` failed `AccountDiscriminatorMismatch` on `claim`; the entire simulated sequence failed. Individual signed simulations supplying every live program-owned account type as `claim` (Pool, Config, Market, 120-byte Position, 114-byte legacy Position) each failed at the same discriminator check.

An unfiltered program-owned-account census at slot 458741977 returned 43,957 accounts: 43,952 Position records, three Market, one Pool, one Config. These are the only observed first-eight-byte discriminators and sizes, so there was no live **yoLov-owned** account with the discriminator accepted by `ProcessPayout` at that slot. This does not identify the required claim type or exclude a claim owned by another program or created ephemerally in one transaction. The assumed `account:Claim` discriminator used in an earlier filtered check was not present in the ELF and must not be treated as the actual type.

## Conclusion and limits

No outsider-accessible net-positive atomic transaction or immediate JupUSD drain was validated. The payout path cannot be reached with any currently live yoLov-owned account type, and the observed public liquidation call transferred no tokens to the caller. The active BTC-market oracle address is bound at runtime. A separate signed interface-gate branch used the same hooked wallet, correct derived position PDA, and an ATA initialized within the simulated transaction; `OpenPosition` at 0, 1, and 5,000,000 raw collateral each failed `keeper ConstraintAddress` with required address `A2rCQdCqQB4wu72x9Q7iq1QugtdZmcyWsiSPfgfZzu2u`. A `ClosePosition` simulation on the hooked wallet's own position, with its ATA initialized in the same simulated transaction, reached the same fixed-keeper gate. These controls are recorded in `yolov-open-corrected-gate.json` and `yolov-close-corrected-gate.json`. A flashloan cannot make these observed no-op, discriminator-failing, and fixed-keeper paths profitable by itself; a composition would need a reachable state-changing payout or close path. This report does not claim exhaustive binary coverage or a general proof of safety.
