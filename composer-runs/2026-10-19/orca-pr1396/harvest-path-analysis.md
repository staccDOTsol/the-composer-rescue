# Can an outsider harvest the PR #1396 residual?

**Finding:** In the examined PR #1396 accounting path, I found a repeatable **loss of an LP's collectible claim**, but no path that credits the lost amount to a different position or pays it to the caller. This is a scoped conclusion about the residual rounding mechanism, not a proof that every possible Whirlpool instruction or unrelated bug is harmless.

## State changed by the outsider update

`UpdateFeesAndRewards` takes no position authority. It calculates current fee/reward growth, then writes the target `Position` checkpoint and owed fields and advances the pool's reward clock/growth. It does not write another position, fee-growth-global, active pool liquidity, tick fee-growth-outside, or protocol fees. Its tick arrays are read-only in the instruction account list, and the liquidity-delta-zero tick helper returns an unchanged tick. [Update handler](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/instructions/update_fees_and_rewards.rs#L9-L43), [growth calculation and position update](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/liquidity_manager.rs#L58-L85), [zero-delta tick behavior](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/tick_manager.rs#L30-L44).

For a fixed-liquidity position, let `Q=2^64`, `L` be its liquidity, `d` be the Q64 growth since its checkpoint, and `q=floor(L·d/Q)` be whole units credited by one successful update. The post-PR `Partial` mode advances its checkpoint by `a=0` if `q=0`, otherwise `a=ceil(q·Q/L)`. Because `q≤L·d/Q` and `d` is an integer, `a≤d`; because `a≥q·Q/L`, `q≤L·a/Q`. Across any sequence of updates with total growth `D`, the sum of consumed checkpoint growth satisfies `Σa≤D`, hence `Σq≤L·D/Q`, and integer `Σq≤floor(L·D/Q)`. Interleaving can **under-credit**, as our real swaps show, but it cannot make this position collect more than one end-only update for the same growth. This argument applies separately to fee A, fee B, and each reward because they use the same helper. It assumes ordinary monotonic growth over the interval and no unrelated overflow or inconsistent tick state. [Checkpoint helper](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/position_manager.rs#L78-L105), [fee and reward application](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/position_manager.rs#L25-L62).

In [the two-position deployed-ELF replay](LIVE-ELF-HIGH-LIQUIDITY.md), the outsider updated the 75% victim LP after each of 20 real swaps. Its collected A fees fell from 30 to 20 raw units. The other 25% LP collected **10 in both schedules**; total fee collection and vault debit fell from 40 to 30. The caller received zero. [Receipt](high-liquidity-live-elf.json).

In a separate [local fork of an existing pool](REAL-POOL-DIFFERENTIAL.md), two identical 20,000-raw-USDC swaps with interim updates left the existing sole LP with one raw USDC unit less `fee_owed_b` than two swaps followed by one update. The swapper and vault token balances were identical in the two branches. That is direct evidence of claim loss without a contemporaneous outsider payout in this specific fork, though it does not test every later instruction or prove a large-value impact.

The same fork test then opened an attacker-owned position *after* that one-unit gap, increased its liquidity by 100,000,000, updated, collected fees, and decreased its full liquidity on both branches. Every signed local transaction succeeded. The actor's `CollectFees` transfer was zero, and its final token A and USDC balances were identical across the branches. Both pool vault balances also matched. The victim retained the one-unit `fee_owed_b` difference. See the [interleaved](real-mainnet-high-liquidity-new-lp-interleave.json) and [end-only](real-mainnet-high-liquidity-new-lp-endOnly.json) receipts. This directly tests the proposed new-LP deposit/withdraw harvest and finds none in that pool state.

The update can advance the pool's shared reward-growth clock, which deserves a separate check. For fixed active pool liquidity and emission rate, and when each checked multiplication succeeds, each reward-clock step adds `floor(elapsedSeconds·emissionsX64/Lpool)` to global reward growth. Splitting one elapsed interval into several such steps cannot increase their sum: `floor(x)+floor(y)≤floor(x+y)`. Liquidity-changing instructions also advance the reward clock before they apply the liquidity change. Thus ordinary extra updates may lose global rounding dust, but this route does not create additional attacker reward growth. A multiplication overflow defaults the increment to zero and is a separate path outside that inequality. [Reward-growth calculation](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/whirlpool_manager.rs#L16-L44), [liquidity-change order](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/liquidity_manager.rs#L101-L114).

## Alternative positioning and instructions

| Proposed route | Why the victim's residual is not credited to the outsider |
| --- | --- |
| Open a new LP position after griefing | A new position starts with zero liquidity; its first nonzero increase uses `Full` mode, which checkpoints existing inside growth before assigning new liquidity. It cannot backdate an LP claim. [Liquidity manager](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/liquidity_manager.rs#L138-L163), [Full mode](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/position_manager.rs#L92-L94). |
| Increase, decrease, or reposition liquidity | These require the position authority/NFT. The prior position liquidity is used to credit accrued growth, then `Full` checkpoints current growth before changing liquidity. Token transfers for liquidity changes depend on `L`, sqrt price, and tick range; deposit token deltas round up and withdrawal deltas round down. They do not read a vault-surplus amount. [Increase authority](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/instructions/increase_liquidity.rs#L25-L33), [decrease authority](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/instructions/decrease_liquidity.rs#L21-L41), [token-delta math](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/liquidity_manager.rs#L192-L223). |
| Cross ticks and return | Tick crossing changes fee-growth-outside as a function of global growth and prior outside growth. It does not read any position checkpoint, so crossing cannot assign the victim's erased checkpoint difference to the attacker's position. A separate tick-math bug would need its own evidence. [Tick crossing](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/tick_manager.rs#L7-L27), [inside-growth calculation](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/tick_manager.rs#L93-L141). |
| Claim rewards | Rewards use the same per-position checkpoint helper. `CollectReward` requires the position authority and transfers no more than that position's recorded owed amount or the reward vault balance. A victim's lost reward claim does not increment another position's owed amount. If a reward vault is underfunded, collection order may affect who receives the limited balance, but that is competition among already-earned claims, not a newly created entitlement. [Reward collection](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/instructions/collect_reward.rs#L11-L83). |
| Collect fees or protocol fees | `CollectFees` transfers exactly the position's `fee_owed` values and requires its authority/NFT. Protocol fee collection requires the configured authority and transfers recorded `protocol_fee_owed`, which the victim update does not increase. [Fee collection](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/instructions/collect_fees.rs#L10-L68), [protocol fee collection](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/instructions/collect_protocol_fees.rs#L5-L51). |

An attacker can own an LP position and collect their own share of fees they generate, including in a flash-funded swap sequence. The victim's checkpoint loss does not increase that share. Swap fee growth is computed from fees paid by the swapper and current active liquidity; it does not take an uncredited-vault-balance term. [Swap fee-growth calculation](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools/blob/f2a3d13fa04eb15cf5b5a309ef9b226fd5d34e36/programs/whirlpool/src/manager/swap_manager.rs#L271-L291). A fair-price swap can transfer tokens out of the vault but must pay the quoted input; the residual does not alter the swap quote.

## Limits

The recurrence proof excludes separately induced `u128` growth wraps, owed-field wraps, malformed tick state, and independent token-math bugs; the code explicitly uses wrapping arithmetic and treats multiplication overflow as lost accrual. We have not identified a feasible sequence in those conditions that turns *this residual* into outsider credit. The only executed differential here is loss of collectible fees with the same other-LP collection. Any assertion of a profitable harvest requires an exact transaction that increases the outsider's token balance **relative to the same market actions without the victim update**, after loan repayment, swap fees, and slippage. No such transaction has been demonstrated.
