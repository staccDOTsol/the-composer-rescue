# Jupiter Lock (`Locp…`) current-binary and atomic-composition audit

Observed 2026-10-15 on Solana mainnet. This is a **bounded audit of 308 escrow vaults** found in the preceding top-holder token-account census, not an enumeration of every Jupiter Lock escrow. All RPC reads and `simulateTransaction` calls were read only. No transaction was signed or submitted. Successful simulations with `sigVerify:false` are counterfactual controls and do **not** establish possession of the creator or recipient keys.

## What controls the tokens

`LocpQgucEQHbqNABEYvBvwoxCPsSbG91A1QaQhQQqjn` is the executable **Jupiter Lock vesting escrow** program, also used by Meteora integrations; it is not Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve program. At confirmed slot 457348063 it was owned by the upgradeable BPF loader, with ProgramData `7yXf8ZG1qdc6GdRUc6LxmwUJuUDUAqta84oPU7fsJ6Yy`, last deploy slot 368684289, and upgrade authority `CvQZZ23qYDWF2RUpxYJ8y9K4skmuvYEEjH7fK58jtipQ`. Its dumped executable is 664,736 bytes, SHA-256 `e0ace82e092741fe0dfe9e5c8ec45dfdbc642bfc510bb9e126f1fb8a6142ab92`; the local DBC `locker.so` fixture has a different hash. The [program and source audit](locp-source-audit.md) records the loader bytes, IDL comparison, state layout, and source-confidence limit. The readable Fogo fork is a reference, **not** a verified build match for the deployed binary.

All 308 sampled authority accounts were exact 296-byte `VestingEscrow` PDAs derived from `["escrow", base]` under Locp, and all 308 token vaults were the matching associated token accounts for the stored mint and token program. The split is 206 SPL Token and 102 Token-2022 vaults. None was cancelled at the snapshot. These are integer vesting schedules with `totalClaimed`; the escrows have no lending shares or debt. [Decoded state and vault proof](locp-source-current-state.json).

For **observed outbound transfers**, [read-only transaction traces](locp-outbound-signer-traces.json) inspected 188 recent successful signatures selected from vaults with more than one successful signature in their latest 40. There were 114 matching outgoing SPL/Token-2022 transfers from 36 distinct sampled vaults, spanning 25 symbols, and every one had Locp as the immediate caller of the token transfer. A recent CMNS example is [signature `BeY3…`](https://solscan.io/tx/BeY3h8qqvFbRAEwanYgErNitdYTCu7PHzi3SkzC6geCWc5Q5cgv5fw6Ad5WpHVhUXGQsTR73JAaoBXeuA6Sn7Ao), slot 456554623: top-level `ClaimV2`, then SPL `transferChecked` at stack height 2 from vault `CFmVuKiy6xtyKtk123ebA2Yw8PRGxC43rTdYvL7k4y7d`, raw amount 16,666,666,666,685, with Locp as its caller. A [DBR example](https://solscan.io/tx/Pp94BjG7zg9EGKKRRw2NGLJDQU2W8gEv6ac7RaVZnypcJLY98NDzrn2oSwibDWB5fCUhxXN5PvBLVKkfJVNc1Bz) has top-level Squads V4, Locp at stack height 2, and the token transfer at height 3, again immediately called by Locp. This history sample does not prove that all 308 vaults have had an outbound transfer; the large YZY vault `oSSQa…` has no observed outgoing transfer in its two sampled signatures. Its current PDA, owner, and vault relationship are established by account bytes.

The deployed instruction gates permit existing-vault outflow through `claim`/`claim_v2` with the **stored recipient signer** and vesting cap, or through `cancel_vesting_escrow` with the stored creator/recipient according to the escrow's cancel-mode bits. Cancellation pays the currently vested share to the stored recipient and remainder to the stored creator, then closes the vault. `close_vesting_escrow` requires the creator and completed claiming unless the escrow was cancelled; recipient updates have their own mode-dependent signer permission. Current-binary direct simulations confirmed outsider claim error 2001 (`ConstraintHasOne`), outsider cancellation/update error 6005 (`NotPermitToDoThisAction`), wrong cancellation payout-owner error 2015, and creator early-close error 6019. [Direct gate results](locp-source-exit-simulations.json).

The claim instruction allows the authorized recipient to name a same-mint destination owned by someone else. On the deployed binary, an unsigned **stored-recipient** YZY claim to an existing **creator-owned** ATA moved exactly 1,000,000 raw YZY from the vault, added 1,000,000 to that ATA, and incremented `totalClaimed` by 1,000,000. Substituting the creator as signer failed 2001; aliasing destination to the vault failed 6006. Two authorized repeated claims moved exactly 2,000,000 total. This is recipient-controlled routing, not a creator or outsider bypass. [Destination simulation](locp-source-claim-destination-simulations.json).

Ten of the 19 sampled escrows with at least $1 million of nominal currently claimable balance have off-curve stored recipients, totaling about **$56.89 million at stale spot marks**. All ten checked as Squads V4 vault addresses, and a successful historic DBR claim shows `SQDS4…` executing `ClaimV2` by CPI with the off-curve recipient **not** a transaction signer. The Squads-controlled vault account is System-owned, so its account owner alone does not identify its PDA signer controller. The other nine have no sampled outbound claim; the classification uses the Squads vault-check endpoint and is not an exhaustive history proof. The three largest claimable escrows, totaling ~$106.85 million at the same marks, have one on-curve YZY recipient. [Recipient provenance and bounded selection](locp-source-recipient-provenance.json).

## Assets and cash-value limit

At confirmed slots 457347564–567, the 308 current vault balances had a **$473,761,103.72 spot mark using prior census prices**. The decoded schedules yielded **$182,589,275.24 nominally claimable by stored recipients** across 156 escrows at that block time. Neither figure is outsider-accessible proceeds or realizable sale value. The four largest mint marks were YZY $202.67 million, DBR $90.15 million, JUP $58.43 million, and WET $41.86 million. The sampled vaults hold 69.7% of YZY mint supply and 77.0% of WET supply. Read-only one-trade USDC quotes found no full-amount route for the ~$117.86 million marked scheduled YZY amount; the ~$37.77 million marked scheduled DBR amount quoted ~$2.78 million out with 92.7% price impact; WET's ~$10.47 million marked scheduled amount quoted ~$353,000 out. These quotes were not built, simulated, or executed, and cannot be summed into a cash total. **No total liquid-dollar value or attacker profit has been established.** [Economics report and exact limitations](locp-economics.md).

## True zero-fee KLend/JUP funding and Solana v1 tests

A read-only [KLend reserve scan](locp-klend-reserve-candidates.json) found three JUP reserves whose **configured** `flash_loan_fee_sf` was exactly `0`; this is a zero fee rate, not a positive rate rounded to zero for dust. It found no matching KLend reserves for YZY, DBR, WET, or CMNS in the same mint-keyed scan. The tested JUP reserve was `4AFAGAm5G8fkcKy7QerL88E7BiSE22ZRbvJzvaKjayor`, with supply vault `4AWAjssbahfbzS7Qs3DJ7588CsCKyNPYcVw9ztENtv23` and **295,382,700,454 raw JUP** available at the snapshot (295,382.700454 JUP). The sampled top JUP lock held 94,414,767,978,779 raw JUP. A [successful mainnet KLend flash pair](locp-jup-flash-sample.json) supplied the actual account metas; a [successful reserve refresh](locp-jup-refresh-sample.json) supplied the refresh metas.

The [25 read-only claim/flash simulations](locp-klend-v1-simulations.json) and [six cancellation/flash simulations](locp-klend-v1-cancel.json) used the deployed programs through `simulateTransaction`, with a serialized **Solana v1 4,096-byte envelope**, no address lookup tables, at most 19 accounts in the reported cases, explicit 1.4 million compute-unit and 64 MiB loaded-data caps, `sigVerify:false`, and fresh blockhash replacement. The tested wires were 404–885 bytes, depending on case. No local size/account gate was substituted for execution. Successful controls compared pre/post token balances and KLend reserve available amount, borrowed amount, and collateral mint supply; failed simulations returned null post-account snapshots and therefore are judged from instruction errors/logs, not null balances.

The stored JUP recipient's pre-cliff `ClaimV2` also returned success with **zero** payout and zero `totalClaimed` change; it was a no-op, not a failed instruction. The corresponding JUP lock permits creator cancellation, which explains the positive authorized cancellation control before the 2027 cliff. The zero-rate flashloan was real in the current reserve state and the atomic compositions fit v1, but it supplied no authority to claim or cancel someone else's escrow. For the tested outsider routes, the first blocker was the stored-signer check; no positive outsider balance or reserve debt/share mismatch appeared in a successful control. This is a bounded current-binary negative result, not a proof that every possible Jupiter Lock account or external integration is safe.

The historical signer traces and read-only simulations are strong evidence for the tested accounts and instruction paths. A live profitable-exit claim would require an actual authorized signer or a demonstrated bypass, a route to withdraw valuable tokens, complete loan repayment, and executable sale proceeds. None was demonstrated here. The program's upgrade authority can change future binary behavior, so these results apply to the observed deployment and state.
