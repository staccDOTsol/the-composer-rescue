## Bounded search for a third zero-fee WSOL provider

- **Save/Solend:** A read-only `getProgramAccounts` scan of a local mainnet-backed fork at slot **458873373**, restricted to 619-byte Save reserves with the exact WSOL mint at byte 42, returned **79** reserves. The flash fee WAD at byte 314 was `500000000000000` on 46 and `3000000000000000` on 33; **none was zero**. The large Save reserve `8PbodeaosQP19SjYFx855UMqWxH2HynZLdBXmsrbac36` is among them. In the [Bison controls](../bison-wsol-flash-envelope.json), a 1,000,000-raw WSOL Save loan raised its fee receiver by 400 raw WSOL; the max-loan control raised it by 31,083,416,735 raw, with the remaining 1 raw sent to the borrower-host fee account. A zero net *principal-vault* delta therefore does not make Save a zero-fee lender.
- **Port and Larix:** A similarly filtered local-fork read at slot **458873538** found four 575-byte Port WSOL reserve accounts and one 873-byte Larix WSOL reserve account (mint byte 42). Decoding each program's reserve `flash_loan_fee_wad` field from its retained source layout ([Port](../../2026-10-15/chnry-usdg-daybreak-provider-port-source/token-lending/program/src/state/reserve.rs), [Larix](../../2026-10-15/chnry-usdg-daybreak-provider-larix-source/src/state/reserve.rs)) gave `3000000000000000` on **all five**. None has an exact-zero configured fee. No loan packet for these reserves was attempted because the configuration alone excludes them from this 0%-only route.
- **Jupiter native flash wrappers:** The isolated wrapper admin `G1qKidJxv6dxBn7TDEx7KSbvovCJQkwzpXbAHQCZxhrn` has a configured 0 bps flash fee, but the [Bison tested borrow position](../bison-wsol-flash-envelope.json) and liquidity vault are for **USDe**, mint `DEkqHyPN7GMRJ5cArtQFAWefqbZb33Hyf6s5iCwjEonT`, not WSOL. A read-only position scan at slot **458873374** filtered Jupiter isolated liquidity program `jup6QF1sNDGpkkcu6F4qaFHcRBmnSS1VgyB4uFbBvNS` to 120-byte `UserBorrowPosition` accounts with WSOL at byte 40 and found one; its decoded current debt ceiling is **0** and max debt ceiling **1 raw unit**, with protocol `ALXWtv2P4GqH1B7Lq731joag52yRBRqmHV4naiXPTYWL` rather than that isolated wrapper admin. The corresponding scan of main liquidity program `jupgfSgfuAXv4B6R2Uxu85Z1qdzgju79s6MfZekN6XS` found **zero** WSOL positions. No material Jupiter WSOL flash capacity is established by these states.
- **A second KLend reserve:** The deployed KLend binary rejects a second flash borrow anywhere in the same transaction with `MultipleFlashBorrows` 6035, even if a different reserve is selected. See the [signed duplicate-borrow control](../../2026-10-15/1oop-current-audit.md) and [KLend/Project0 composition report](../jup-v103-audit/dual-flash-report.md). A second reserve cannot provide an additional leg in the same v1 transaction.

This is a bounded inventory of the named providers and current forked accounts, not a Solana-wide proof that a third WSOL zero-fee provider does not exist. Forks and live liquidity can change; any later maximum must be checked against fresh reserve config, lender balances, and the full actor/vault accounting in the same signed transaction.
