## secrets.fun hydration error #418 on `/` was `timeAgo()` text rendered at two different clocks (2026-10-19)

**Why:** any new server-rendered relative time must use `useNow()`, not `Date.now()`; and a hydration error that "always fires" can depend on how old the newest event is.
