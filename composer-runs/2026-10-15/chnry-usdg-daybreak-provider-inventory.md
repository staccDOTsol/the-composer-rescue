# Chancery exact USDG/USDv flash provider inventory — 2026-10-15

## Decision and scope

The bounded mainnet scan found **two distinct, currently usable Jupiter Lend native flash deployments for the exact USDG mint** `2u1tszSeqZ3qBWF3uNGPFc8TzMk2tdiwknnRMWGWjGWH`, in addition to the already examined KLend/Kamino and Project0/marginfi routes. The Jupiter **main** and **isolated Ethena** wrappers bind different liquidity programs, reserves, USDG vaults, flash admins, and borrow positions. Both currently configure an **exact zero basis point flash fee**, support the Token-2022 USDG mint, and passed a signed Solana v1 one-cycle flash borrow/payback check on a local fork. Current practical borrow size is limited by live lending utilization and each flash wrapper's designated position debt ceiling, rather than by the displayed token vault balance alone.

The same bounded scan found **no USDv reserve or bank in either Jupiter deployment, Save/Solend, Port, Larix, Mango v4, or Drift** for exact USDv mint `USDvUSpnhCr9yBgj3UyVrD239HRUv4RsHwH2FxsWuMk`. This is a statement about those named lenders at the observed slots, **not** a claim that USDv is absent from Solana or that every possible flash protocol was inventoried. USDv does sit in swap pools; those balances are not validated same-mint flash capacity.

All live RPC access here was read-only. The direct provider probes used mainnet `simulateTransaction` with `sigVerify:false` and never submitted a transaction. Separate local mainnet-fork harnesses signed real v1 wire transactions and called only fork `simulateTransaction` with `sigVerify:true`; they did not submit a live transaction. No main audit report was modified.

They **do not** establish a fully signed successful authorized-operator Chancery conversion: the authorized operator's private key was unavailable. ## Swap venues and coverage limit

The [top-account sample](chnry-usdg-daybreak-provider-token-top.json) found a USDG vault of about **8.90 million** in an Orca Whirlpool and USDv vaults of about **1.319 million**, **316,641**, and **100,535** in Raydium CLMM pools at slot 457360278. These are AMM swap inventories. The [official Orca Whirlpools repository](https://github.com/orca-so/whirlpools), [Raydium CLMM repository](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm), and [Meteora DLMM instruction inventory](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/docs/blob/main/developer-guides/dlmm/program/instructions.mdx) describe swap/liquidity surfaces; this check did not validate a same-mint flash borrow/payback primitive against those pools. Jupiter routing through AMMs likewise does not turn their balances into flash credit. AMM liquidity could matter to a **swap-funded** route, but that route has different assets, slippage, and repayment constraints and was not established here.

This inventory covers the named Jupiter, Save, Port, Larix, Mango v4, and Drift deployments plus the bounded AMM distinction above. It is **not a Solana-wide program census**. Unknown or newer lenders, alternate deployments, cross-protocol combinations, and USDv swap routes remain unassessed. The live USDG capacity samples and zero-fee settings should be refreshed immediately before any later review because governance settings, utilization, and vault balances can change.
